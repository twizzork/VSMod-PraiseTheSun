# twizzork's Praise The Sun

Vintage Story mod to make it harder for drifters, shivers, and bowtorn to spawn in low light (light level 4, down from regular 7)

## Install

One click from the VS Mod DB, https://mods.vintagestory.at/tzpraisethesun

Or: drag & drop `tzpraisethesun` zip into your VS mods folder (win: `%APPDATA%\VintagestoryData\Mods`)

> [!NOTE]
> to find your mod folder: open VS to main menu, click "Mod Manager", then click the button at the bottom to "open mods folder"
